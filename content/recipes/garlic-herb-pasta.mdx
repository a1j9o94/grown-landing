## Why This Recipe Works

Garlic herb pasta is the perfect introduction to cooking with THC oil. The technique is forgiving, the timing is clear, and the fat-rich sauce helps with THC absorption.

The secret? **Add the THC oil completely off heat.** This preserves full potency while letting the residual warmth of the pasta integrate everything into a silky sauce.

## Notes on Dosing

This recipe yields 4 portions at approximately **7.5mg THC per serving** when using 2 tablespoons of Grown THC Oil.

For a lighter experience:
- Use 1 tablespoon of THC oil (15mg total, ~4mg per serving)
- Or finish with THC salt instead of oil

For guests who don't want THC:
- Portion their pasta before adding THC oil
- Keep a small bowl of regular olive oil on hand

## Serving Suggestions

Pair with:
- A simple arugula salad with lemon vinaigrette
- Crusty bread (regular olive oil for dipping—don't waste your THC oil!)
- A light white wine
