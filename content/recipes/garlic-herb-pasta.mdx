## Why This Recipe Works

This bowl hits the sweet spot for THC cooking: **bold flavor, short cook time, and a clear “finishing moment.”** Lemon, garlic, and tomato cover any herbal notes, and couscous makes it feel like a full meal without effort.

The key technique is simple: **cook the shrimp normally, then add THC oil after heat is off.**

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **THC Oil:** best as a finishing drizzle once the pan is off heat (or directly on bowls).
- **THC Salt:** best as a final sprinkle at the table.

This keeps the base recipe totally normal—and makes it easy to serve a mixed group.

## Notes on Dosing

Use your product label as the source of truth. If you’re hosting, the safest approach is:
- **Offer THC as an optional finishing step** (oil drizzle or pinch of salt).
- **Encourage a slow pace**—edibles can take a while to show up.
- **Never pressure anyone** to match someone else’s serving.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add sliced cucumbers or a quick Greek salad vibe (feta, olives).
- Swap couscous for quick rice or quinoa if you prefer.
- Finish with extra lemon zest for maximum brightness.
