## Why This Recipe Works

This bowl hits the sweet spot for THC cooking: **bold flavor, short cook time, and a clear finishing moment.** Lemon, garlic, and tomato carry the dish, and couscous makes it a full meal without effort.

The key technique is simple: **cook the shrimp normally, then finish each bowl with a sachet.**

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Seasoning Salt:** one sachet per bowl, sprinkled at the table.
- **Chili-Lime Seasoning:** the hotter, brighter swap, same one-sachet move.

This keeps the base recipe totally normal and makes a mixed group easy to serve.

## Notes on Dosing

The sachet is the source of truth: one sachet = one 5mg serving. If you are hosting, the safest approach is:
- **Offer THC as an optional finishing step.**
- **Encourage a slow pace.** Edibles can take a while to show up.
- **Never pressure anyone** to match someone else's serving.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add sliced cucumbers or a quick Greek salad vibe (feta, olives).
- Swap couscous for quick rice or quinoa if you prefer.
- Finish with extra lemon zest for maximum brightness.
