## Why This Recipe Works

Shrimp cooks in minutes and takes bold seasoning well. Cold slaw and cool crema balance the heat, and the blend lands on top at the end where you can see and control it.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Chili-Lime Seasoning:** Dust one sachet (5mg THC) across a plate of tacos, to taste, rather than treating it as one sachet per taco. Anyone skipping THC gets a plain plate.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is planned as one 5mg THC serving. Formulation is still in development.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
