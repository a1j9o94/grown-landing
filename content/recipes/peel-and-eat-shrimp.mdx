## Why This Recipe Works

Boiling the seasoning into the water is the usual move, but it spreads it evenly and you cannot take it back. Steaming plain shrimp and seasoning at the table gives each person control, and the blend still lands on every bite.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Seafood Seasoning:** Give each guest one sachet (5mg THC) and let them dust their own pile, tasting as they go. The base shrimp stay THC-free, so mixed tables stay easy.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is planned as one 5mg THC serving. Formulation is still in development.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
