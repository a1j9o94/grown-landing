## Why This Recipe Works

This one is all about **contrast**: salty-sweet filling, sharp lime, crunchy lettuce, and bright pickles. It tastes complete without THC, and the THC integration happens at the very end via sauce or finishing salt.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **THC Oil:** best in a finishing sauce drizzled per wrap.
- **THC Salt:** best as a final sprinkle per wrap.

## Notes on Dosing

Because wraps are individual units, you can keep things very clear:
- Offer THC sauce/salt as an optional finish.
- Label THC components clearly.
- Encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a simple coconut rice if you want to make it heartier.
- Swap cucumber pickles for quick carrot ribbons or radish slices.
