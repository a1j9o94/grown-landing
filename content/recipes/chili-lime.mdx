## Why This Recipe Works

This one is all about **contrast**: salty-sweet filling, sharp lime, crunchy lettuce, and bright pickles. It tastes complete without THC, and the THC integration happens at the very end, wrap by wrap.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Chili-Lime Seasoning:** dust each wrap just before eating. The blend was practically built for this dish.
- **Seasoning Salt:** the savory alternative, sprinkled over the filling.

## Notes on Dosing

Because wraps are individual units, you can keep things very clear:
- One sachet per person, split across their wraps.
- Label the sachet bowl clearly.
- Encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a simple coconut rice if you want to make it heartier.
- Swap cucumber pickles for quick carrot ribbons or radish slices.
