## Why This Recipe Works

Crab cakes are delicate. Mixing a seasoning into the batter means every cake has the same amount, whether the eater wants it or not. Finishing at the table keeps the crab sweet and puts the choice on the plate.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Seafood Seasoning:** Keep the cakes plain and finish each plate with one sachet (5mg THC), sprinkled across the cakes. The blend stays out of the batter so you can portion it exactly.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is one 5mg THC serving.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
