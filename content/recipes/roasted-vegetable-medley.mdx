## The Technique

Roasting vegetables at high heat develops incredible flavor through caramelization. But that same high heat would degrade THC if the seasoning went in before roasting.

The solution: **roast with regular oil, finish with Grown.**

This gives you the best of both worlds: deeply caramelized, flavorful vegetables with full THC potency preserved.

## Timing Tips

1. Roast vegetables normally
2. Portion onto plates while still warm
3. Sprinkle one sachet per THC serving
4. Toss gently on the plate

The residual warmth blooms the herbs in the blend without any potency loss.

## Portioning for Parties

At one sachet (5mg) per serving, this is a moderate dose appropriate for most guests. To adjust:

**Lower dose:** split a sachet between two portions (about 2.5mg each)

**No dose:** leave the sachet off. The vegetables are fully seasoned without it.

## Serving Suggestions

These vegetables are versatile:
- As a side to grilled chicken or steak
- Tossed with pasta
- Over grain bowls with tahini
- Alongside eggs for elevated brunch

## Make It a Meal

For a complete THC dinner party:
- **Starter**: Simple salad (no THC, let guests warm up)
- **Main**: Grilled protein (no THC)
- **Side**: These vegetables (THC)
- **Dessert**: Chili-lime dark chocolate truffles (THC)

Total THC: about 10mg per guest (5mg vegetables + 5mg truffles)
