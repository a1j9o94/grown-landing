## The Technique

Roasting vegetables at high heat develops incredible flavor through caramelization. But that same high heat would destroy THC if added before roasting.

The solution: **roast with regular oil, finish with THC oil.**

This gives you the best of both worlds—deeply caramelized, flavorful vegetables with full THC potency preserved.

## Timing Tips

1. Roast vegetables normally
2. Remove from oven, let rest 3-5 minutes
3. The vegetables should still be warm but not steaming
4. Add THC oil and toss gently

The residual warmth helps the oil coat evenly without degrading THC.

## Portioning for Parties

At 5mg per serving, this is a moderate dose appropriate for most guests. To adjust:

**Lower dose (2.5mg per serving):**
- Use 1 tbsp THC oil instead of 2

**Higher dose (7.5mg per serving):**
- Use 3 tbsp THC oil
- Or add THC salt finish after the oil

## Serving Suggestions

These vegetables are versatile:
- As a side to grilled chicken or steak
- Tossed with pasta (the oil does double duty)
- Over grain bowls with tahini
- Alongside eggs for elevated brunch

## Make It a Meal

For a complete THC dinner party:
- **Starter**: Simple salad (no THC, let guests warm up)
- **Main**: Grilled protein (no THC)
- **Side**: These vegetables (THC)
- **Dessert**: Salted chocolate truffles (THC salt)

Total THC: 7-10mg per guest (5mg vegetables + 2-4mg truffles)
