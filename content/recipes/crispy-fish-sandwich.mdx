## Why This Recipe Works

Fried fish wants salt, acid and a little heat the moment it comes out of the oil. Seasoning at that point keeps the blend bright and the crust crisp, and the rest of the sandwich stays the same for everyone.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Seafood Seasoning:** Open the sandwich and dust the hot fish with one sachet (5mg THC) before the slaw goes on. The seasoning sticks best to freshly fried fish.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is planned as one 5mg THC serving. Formulation is still in development.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
