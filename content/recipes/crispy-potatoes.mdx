## Why This Recipe Works

This is the cleanest possible THC integration pattern: **keep the main food normal, and put THC into a dip.** It’s easy to portion, easy to opt out of, and it keeps the crispy potatoes exactly how they should be.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **THC Oil:** mix into the dip (best control).
- **THC Salt:** finish the potatoes right before serving.

## Notes on Dosing

Dips are naturally “self-serve,” so keep it responsible:
- Put THC dip in a **clearly labeled** bowl.
- Encourage slow pacing—effects can take a while.
- Keep a non-THC dip option available.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a quick cucumber salad for something fresh.
- Use leftover dip on sandwiches, wraps, or roasted veggies.
