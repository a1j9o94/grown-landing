## Why This Recipe Works

This is the cleanest possible THC integration pattern: **keep the main food normal, and finish each plate with a sachet.** It is easy to portion, easy to opt out of, and it keeps the crispy potatoes exactly how they should be.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Seasoning Salt:** sprinkle one sachet (5mg) over each hot plate right before serving.
- **At the table:** hand each guest their own sachet so they control their serving.

## Notes on Dosing

Sachets make the math disappear:
- One sachet = one 5mg serving.
- Label THC-finished plates clearly.
- Keep plenty of unseasoned potatoes for anyone skipping THC.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a quick cucumber salad for something fresh.
- Use leftover dip on sandwiches, wraps, or roasted veggies.
