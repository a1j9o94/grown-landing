## Why This Recipe Works

Hot wings are the best carrier for dry seasoning, which clings to the oils on the surface. Cooking the wings plain keeps the platter easy to share.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Chili-Lime Seasoning:** Hand each THC guest one sachet (5mg THC) and a small plate of wings to dust, to taste. Keep the main platter plain.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is one 5mg THC serving.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
