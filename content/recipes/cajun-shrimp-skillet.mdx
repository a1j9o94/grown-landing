## Why This Recipe Works

Shrimp cook fast and take bold seasoning well. Finishing at the table puts the blend on top where you can see it, keeps heat out of it, and lets each person decide how much they want.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Cajun Seasoning:** Cook everything plain. Give each THC guest one sachet (5mg THC) to sprinkle over their own plate, to taste. Keep a plain plate for anyone skipping THC.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is planned as one 5mg THC serving. Formulation is still in development.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
