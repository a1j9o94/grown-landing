## Why This Recipe Works

Hot potatoes are the best carrier for dry seasoning. Individual bowls keep portions clear and the shared tray THC-free.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Cajun Seasoning:** Serve wedges in individual bowls and let each THC guest toss one sachet (5mg THC) over their own, to taste.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is planned as one 5mg THC serving. Formulation is still in development.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
