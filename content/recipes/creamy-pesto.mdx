## Why This Recipe Works

Pesto is already fatty and aromatic, which makes it a natural match for a herby seasoning salt. Blistered tomatoes add sweetness and depth, so the whole dish tastes complete with minimal effort.

The technique is the whole game: **cook the pasta normally, finish each bowl with a sachet.**

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- Stir one sachet into each bowl **after plating** (the residual heat wakes up the herbs).
- Or pass sachets at the table and let guests finish their own bowls.

## Notes on Dosing

Because pasta is shareable, treat this as a "choose your own finish" situation:
- Keep the main pan non-THC.
- One sachet per THC bowl, no measuring.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a simple arugula salad with lemon vinaigrette.
- Toss in spinach at the end for a one-pan greens upgrade.
- Top with toasted pine nuts or walnuts for crunch.
