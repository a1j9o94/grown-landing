## Why This Recipe Works

Pesto is already fatty and aromatic, which makes it a natural match for finishing with THC oil. Blistered tomatoes add sweetness and depth, which helps the whole dish taste “complete” with minimal effort.

The technique is the whole game: **turn off the heat, pause briefly, then add THC oil.**

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- Stir THC oil into the pasta **after heat is off** (or drizzle per bowl).
- Use THC salt as a **finishing salt** at the table.

## Notes on Dosing

Because pasta is shareable, this recipe is best handled like a “choose your own finish” situation:
- Keep the main pan non-THC.
- Let each person add THC oil/salt at the end based on the label guidance.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a simple arugula salad with lemon vinaigrette.
- Toss in spinach at the end for a one-pan greens upgrade.
- Top with toasted pine nuts or walnuts for crunch.
