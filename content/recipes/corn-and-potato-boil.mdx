## Why This Recipe Works

Butter makes dry seasoning cling. Buttering first, then dusting at the table, gives corn and potatoes the classic flavor without putting THC in the shared pot.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Seafood Seasoning:** Everything is cooked plain. Give each THC guest one sachet (5mg THC) to dust over their portion after buttering, so the seasoning sticks.

## Notes on Dosing

- Each sachet is one 5mg THC serving.
- Cook the dish plain and season at the table, so anyone can opt out.
- Label THC plates clearly and encourage slow pacing.

## Serving Suggestions

- Keep a plain version on the table for guests who skip THC.
- Serve with something cold and fresh to balance the dish.
