## Why THC Salt Works Here

These truffles showcase the unique benefits of THC salt:

1. **Faster onset** — Salt dissolves on your tongue, allowing some THC to absorb sublingually
2. **Precise dosing** — Each pinch is approximately 2mg, easy to count
3. **Visual appeal** — Flake salt crystals catch the light beautifully
4. **Flavor contrast** — The salt-chocolate combination is a classic for a reason

## The Salt Technique

Timing is everything:
- Add salt **just before serving**
- The crystals should remain visible on top
- Don't roll the salted truffles or press the salt in

When guests eat the truffle, the salt will dissolve on their tongue first, providing that faster sublingual absorption before the chocolate follows.

## Dosing Notes

Each truffle with one pinch of THC salt contains approximately **2mg THC**.

For a typical experience:
- **Light dose**: 1-2 truffles (2-4mg)
- **Moderate dose**: 3-4 truffles (6-8mg)
- **Strong dose**: 5+ truffles (10mg+)

Effects from the salt may begin in 15-30 minutes—faster than typical edibles.

## Make-Ahead Strategy

These truffles are perfect for entertaining:
- Make the ganache up to 1 week ahead
- Roll truffles the day before
- Add salt immediately before serving

The only step that can't be done ahead? The finishing salt.

## Presentation Tips

- Arrange on a slate or dark plate for contrast
- Use a variety of finishing salts if you have them (regular flake salt alongside THC salt)
- Provide small cards indicating which are THC-infused
- Keep count of how many each guest takes
