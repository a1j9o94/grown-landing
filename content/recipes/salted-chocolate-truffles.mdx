## Why This Recipe Works

Chocolate is a natural cover flavor: deep, bitter, and aromatic. Chili and lime with dark chocolate is a pairing with centuries behind it, so the seasoning reads as a chef's choice, not a compromise. And everything happens off heat, so there is no temperature or timing fight.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **Chili-Lime Seasoning:** dust each serving right before it goes out. Half a sachet (2.5mg) for a gentle finish, a full sachet (5mg) for a standard serving.
- Keep a plain cocoa-only plate for anyone skipping THC.

## Notes on Dosing

For desserts, clarity matters more than cleverness:
- Keep THC components optional and clearly labeled.
- Serve THC-dusted truffles on their own plate, never mixed with the plain batch.
- Encourage patience. Edibles can be slow.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a platter with berries, salted nuts, and espresso.
- Try rolling some truffles in crushed pistachios for texture.
