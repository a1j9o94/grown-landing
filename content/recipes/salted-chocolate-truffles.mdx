## Why This Recipe Works

Chocolate is a natural “cover” flavor—deep, bitter, and aromatic—so it pairs beautifully with infused oil. These truffles also have the best THC integration property: **everything happens off heat**, so you’re not fighting temperature or timing.

## THC Option: Where to Add It

- **THC Oil:** stir in once ganache is smooth and fully off heat.
- **THC Salt:** a tiny finishing pinch right before serving.

## Notes on Dosing

For desserts, clarity matters more than cleverness:
- Keep THC components optional and clearly labeled.
- If serving guests, consider keeping a non-THC batch and a separate THC-finished batch.
- Encourage patience—edibles can be slow.

## Serving Suggestions

- Add a platter with berries, salted nuts, and espresso.
- Try rolling some truffles in crushed pistachios for texture.
