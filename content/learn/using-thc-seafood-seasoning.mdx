## Why Finish at the Table?

A bay-style blend is traditionally cooked into the water or the batter. That spreads it evenly, but you cannot take it back and you cannot tell who got what. Grown Seafood Seasoning is made the other way around: cook the food plain, then finish each plate with a sachet.

Each single-serve sachet is 5mg of THC, so the last step is also the simplest one.

## What You Get

### Grown Seafood Seasoning (working name)

- A bay-style blend of celery, paprika and pepper
- Single-serve sachet, 5mg THC
- Sold in 5-packs
- Meant to be sprinkled at the table, to taste

## Dosing with Seafood Seasoning

| Application | Sachets | Total THC |
|-------------|---------|-----------|
| Standard serving | 1 | 5mg |

| Experienced serving | 2 | 10mg |

## Best Applications for Seafood Seasoning

### Perfect Pairings

1. **Shrimp** - steamed, boiled or grilled, seasoned at the table
2. **Crab cakes** - dusted over the top just before eating
3. **Fried fish** - on the fillet right out of the pan
4. **Fries and potatoes** - while they are still hot
5. **Corn on the cob** - after buttering
6. **Deviled eggs and egg salad** - a pinch over the top
7. **Bloody Marys and savory rims** - on the glass, not in the drink

### When NOT to Use It

- Before roasting, frying, or grilling (heat degrades THC)
- In cooking water (it disperses and the dose is lost)
- Mixed into batters or doughs
- In dishes that will be reheated
- Anywhere you cannot control who eats what

## Tips for Entertaining

### The Pass-the-Sachet Method

The cleanest way to serve a mixed group:
1. Cook the whole meal with regular seasoning
2. Put sachets on the table in a clearly labeled dish
3. Each guest seasons their own plate, or skips it

This empowers guests to:
- Choose their dose (half or full sachet)
- Skip THC entirely if preferred
- See exactly what everyone else is having

### Clear Communication

Always announce:
- "These sachets contain THC"
- The dose per sachet (5mg)
- Which dishes, if any, already contain THC

Never surprise your guests.

## Flavor and Texture

Grown Seafood Seasoning is a bay-style blend built to finish seafood and everyday sides:

- **Warm spice**: celery, paprika and pepper in the style of a classic crab boil
- **Made for seafood**: shrimp, crab, fish and fries
- **No "weedy" taste**: it tastes like a seasoning

The sachet is one serving. Sprinkle it across the plate, to taste.

## Storage

- Keep sachets sealed until use
- Store the box in a cool, dry place
- Keep away from the stove and sink (moisture is the enemy)
- Always out of reach of children and pets
