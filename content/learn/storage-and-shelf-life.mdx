## Why Storage Matters

Proper storage protects two things:

1. **Potency** - THC degrades over time, especially with exposure to light, heat, and air
2. **Freshness** - seasoning blends lose their aroma, and moisture causes clumping

Store your Grown sachets correctly, and they will keep their flavor and potency longer.

## Sachet Storage

### Ideal Conditions

- **Temperature:** Cool room temperature (60-75°F)
- **Light:** Store in a dark cupboard or pantry
- **Air and moisture:** Keep sachets sealed until the moment you use them

### The Moisture Problem

Seasoning blends are hygroscopic - they pull moisture from the air. Moisture causes:
- Clumping
- Loss of aroma
- Faster degradation of the blend

**Prevention:**
- Keep sachets sealed until use
- Store the box away from the stove, dishwasher, and sink
- In humid months, a sealed pantry cupboard beats an open shelf

### What Degrades THC

| Factor | Impact | Solution |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Light | Breaks down THC | Dark storage |
| Heat | Accelerates degradation | Cool location |
| Air | Oxidizes cannabinoids | Keep sachets sealed |
| Time | Gradual potency loss | Use within the best-by window |

### Shelf Life

The sealed sachet is the whole point: single-serve packaging keeps air and moisture out until the moment of use.

- **Unopened sachets:** shelf life will be set from stability testing before launch
- **Opened sachets:** use immediately. A torn sachet loses aroma and potency fast

Over time, potency and flavor may decline. Follow the date on your box once one is printed.

### Signs of a Compromised Sachet

- A torn, punctured, or unsealed sachet
- Clumped or damp seasoning
- Faded aroma
- Noticeably weaker effects than expected

If a sachet is damaged, discard it.

## Childproofing & Safety

This is non-negotiable. THC products must be stored:

- **Out of reach of children and pets**
- **In original, labeled packaging**
- **Separate from regular cooking ingredients**
- **In a locked cabinet if children are present**

### The Sachet Rule

A sachet on the counter looks like something from a meal kit. That is exactly why it cannot live on the counter:
- Never leave sachets out during a party
- Keep the box on a dedicated "THC shelf," out of sight
- When serving, hand sachets to guests directly rather than leaving the box on the table

### Guest Awareness

If you have guests who might access your kitchen:
- Store THC products completely out of sight
- Brief guests on what is in your kitchen
- Keep sachets away from ordinary seasoning and condiments

## Travel & Transport

### Can You Travel with THC Products?

**Within Illinois:** Yes, in your vehicle, within legal possession limits
**Across state lines:** No - illegal regardless of destination state laws
**On airplanes:** No - federal jurisdiction applies

### If Transporting Locally

- Keep sachets in their original, sealed box
- Keep the box in the trunk or a locked compartment
- Don't open sachets while driving
