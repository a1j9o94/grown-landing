## Why Finishing Salt?

Finishing salt is not for cooking. It is for *completing*. That final sprinkle on a seared steak, the crystals on sliced tomatoes, the savoriness that wakes up a bowl of pasta. It is the moment where salt is not background seasoning; it is the last decision you make about a dish.

Grown Seasoning Salt brings this experience to THC. Each single-serve sachet delivers a savory, herby blend with exactly 5mg of THC, so the last decision is also the easiest one.

## How the Sachet Works

### Grown Seasoning Salt

- **5mg THC per sachet**
- **About 3g of seasoning per sachet (roughly 1/2 teaspoon)**
- **5 sachets per box**

One sachet seasons one plate generously. Tear, sprinkle, done.

### Why Single-Serve Matters

A jar of infused salt forces you to measure and guess. A sachet does not:
- The dose is always exact
- The count is always visible (sachets used = servings served)
- Nobody accidentally over-seasons with THC
- The rest of the box stays sealed and fresh

## Best Applications for Seasoning Salt

### Perfect Pairings

1. **Eggs** - fried, poached, or soft-scrambled
2. **Roasted vegetables** - after they come out of the oven
3. **Pasta and grain bowls** - stirred through the finished bowl
4. **Grilled meats** - steak, lamb chops, pork tenderloin
5. **Avocado toast** - classic brunch upgrade
6. **Potatoes** - crispy, smashed, or fries
7. **Fresh tomatoes** - with olive oil and basil
8. **Popcorn** - movie night, elevated

### When NOT to Use It

- Before roasting, frying, or grilling (heat degrades THC)
- In cooking water (it disperses and the dose is lost)
- Mixed into batters or doughs
- In dishes that will be reheated
- Anywhere you cannot control who eats what

## Tips for Entertaining

### The Pass-the-Sachet Method

The cleanest way to serve a mixed group:
1. Cook the whole meal with regular seasoning
2. Put sachets on the table in a clearly labeled dish
3. Each guest seasons their own plate, or skips it

This empowers guests to:
- Choose their dose (half or full sachet)
- Skip THC entirely if preferred
- See exactly what everyone else is having

### Clear Communication

Always announce:
- "These sachets contain THC"
- The dose per sachet (5mg)
- Which dishes, if any, already contain THC

Never surprise your guests.

Grown Seasoning Salt is a savory, herby blend built to behave like the finishing salt you already reach for:

- **Savory depth**: enough to finish a dish on its own

The THC is evenly distributed through the blend during production, so the whole sachet is the serving. Sprinkle it all.

## Storage

- Keep sachets sealed until use
- Store the box in a cool, dry place
- Keep away from the stove and sink (moisture is the enemy)
- Always out of reach of children and pets
