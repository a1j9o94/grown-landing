## Why Finishing Salt?

Finishing salt isn't for cooking—it's for *completing*. That final pinch on a seared steak, the crystals atop a chocolate chip cookie, the texture on sliced tomatoes with olive oil. It's the moment where salt isn't seasoning; it's a featured ingredient.

Grown Flake Salt brings this experience to THC. Each delicate crystal delivers fast-acting cannabinoids with a dose you can see and measure.

## How THC Salt Works Differently

Unlike THC oil, which gets absorbed through your digestive system, THC salt offers a unique advantage: **sublingual absorption**.

When salt crystals dissolve on your tongue, some THC enters your bloodstream directly through the mucous membranes in your mouth. This means:

- **Faster onset**: 15-30 minutes vs. 45-90 minutes for digestive absorption
- **Shorter peak**: Effects may not last as long as traditional edibles
- **More immediate feedback**: You can gauge how you feel more quickly

This makes THC salt ideal for micro-dosing and social situations where you want quicker, more predictable effects.

## Dosing with THC Salt

### Grown Flake Salt

- **2mg THC per pinch** (approximately 1/4 teaspoon)
- **60+ servings per jar**

### What's a "Pinch"?

A pinch is what you can hold between your thumb and two fingers. With Grown Flake Salt, we've calibrated the crystal size and THC concentration so that a natural finishing pinch delivers approximately 2mg.

This micro-dose approach is intentional:
- Easy to add more if desired
- Difficult to accidentally over-consume
- Perfect for sharing across multiple dishes

### Dose Building

| Application | Pinches | Total THC |
|-------------|---------|-----------|
| Light finish on appetizer | 1 | 2mg |
| Generous finish on entrée | 2-3 | 4-6mg |
| Dessert topping | 1-2 | 2-4mg |

## Best Applications for THC Salt

### Perfect Pairings

1. **Chocolate desserts** – Brownies, cookies, truffles, mousse
2. **Caramel and toffee** – Sweet-salty combinations shine
3. **Fresh tomatoes** – With olive oil and basil
4. **Grilled meats** – Steak, lamb chops, pork tenderloin
5. **Roasted vegetables** – After they come out of the oven
6. **Avocado toast** – Classic brunch upgrade
7. **Eggs** – Fried, poached, or soft-scrambled
8. **Cocktail rims** – Margaritas, bloody marys (with caution)

### When NOT to Use THC Salt

- In cooking water (it dissolves and disperses unevenly)
- In recipes where salt is mixed into batter or dough
- When you can't control who eats what
- In dishes that will be reheated

## Tips for Entertaining

### The Two-Bowl Method

When serving THC salt at a dinner party:
1. Keep regular finishing salt in one bowl
2. Keep THC salt in a clearly labeled separate container
3. Offer guests the choice to finish their own dishes

This empowers guests to:
- Choose their dose
- Skip THC entirely if preferred
- Add more if they're comfortable

### Clear Communication

Always announce:
- "This salt contains THC"
- The approximate dose per pinch (2mg)
- Whether the dish already contains THC oil

Never surprise your guests.

## Texture and Flavor

Grown Flake Salt is made from harvested grey sea salt, chosen for:

- **Flake structure**: Large, pyramid-shaped crystals that crunch
- **Mineral complexity**: Subtle briny notes without bitterness
- **Melt-on-contact**: Dissolves quickly when it hits moisture
- **Visual appeal**: Beautiful on any finished plate

The THC is bound to the salt during production, so there's no "weedy" taste—just clean, bright salinity with a slight mineral finish.

## Storage

- Keep in a cool, dry place
- Moisture is the enemy—don't store near the stove
- The jar is designed to minimize humidity exposure
- Use within 12 months for best quality
