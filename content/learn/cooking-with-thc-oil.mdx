## Introduction

THC-infused cooking oil is one of the most versatile ingredients in the cannabis kitchen. Unlike baked edibles or gummies, oil integrates seamlessly into your existing cooking routine—you can sauté, roast, drizzle, and dress just as you would with any premium olive oil.

The key difference? Precision. With Grown Heirloom Oil, every teaspoon delivers exactly 5mg of THC, making it easy to calculate doses for yourself and your guests.

## Understanding Heat and THC

One of the most common questions about cooking with THC oil: **does heat destroy THC?**

The short answer is: it depends on time and temperature.

### Safe Cooking Temperatures

THC begins to degrade at temperatures above **320°F (160°C)**. However, most cooking methods don't expose the oil to these temperatures for long enough to cause significant loss:

- **Sautéing at medium heat**: Safe. The oil rarely exceeds 300°F.
- **Roasting vegetables at 400°F**: Safe, if adding oil after roasting or drizzling on finished dishes.
- **Deep frying**: Not recommended. Sustained high heat will degrade THC.
- **Baking**: Generally safe at 350°F or below.

### The Golden Rule

**When in doubt, add the oil at the end.** Finishing a dish with THC oil—drizzling over pasta, tossing with roasted vegetables, or whisking into a vinaigrette—preserves potency and gives you more control.

## Best Uses for THC Oil

### Ideal Applications

1. **Finishing drizzles** – Over pasta, risotto, grilled proteins
2. **Salad dressings** – Whisked with vinegar and herbs
3. **Dipping sauces** – Mixed with balsamic or served with bread
4. **Cold preparations** – Pestos, aiolis, compound butters
5. **Low-heat sautéing** – Garlic and vegetables at medium heat

### What to Avoid

- Deep frying
- High-heat searing
- Extended oven roasting at 400°F+
- Recipes where oil is the only fat (harder to control dose)

## Tips for Dinner Parties

1. **Label everything clearly** – Never serve THC dishes without disclosure
2. **Offer alternatives** – Always have a non-infused version available
3. **Start low, go slow** – Especially with new guests
4. **Time your courses** – Edibles take 30-90 minutes to onset
5. **Plan the evening** – Ensure guests can get home safely

## Storage & Shelf Life

- Store in a cool, dark place
- Keep away from direct sunlight
- Refrigeration extends freshness but isn't required
- Use within 6 months of opening for best potency
