## Introduction

THC-infused seasoning is the easiest way to bring cannabis to the table. No infusions, no math, no special equipment. Each Grown sachet is designed as a single-serve blend with a 5mg THC target, so a serving is always a serving.

The key difference from every other edible format? **You season at the end, per plate.** The dish itself stays exactly the way you already make it.

## The Sachet Format

- **5mg THC target per sachet**
- **Fill weight still in development**

Two blends at launch:
- **Cajun Seasoning** - a savory Cajun-style blend for chicken, potatoes, vegetables, and shrimp
- **Chili-Lime Seasoning** - heat and citrus, built for rims, elote, fruit, and the grill

## Understanding Heat and THC

One of the most common questions: **what does heat do to the seasoning?**

The short answer: it has not been tested yet, so Grown seasoning is designed as a finisher and the label comes first.

### Safe and Unsafe

- **Sprinkling on a warm plate**: The intended use.
- **Tossing through a dish off heat**: Intended use.
- **Seasoning before roasting, frying, or grilling**: Not recommended. Heat effects are untested.
- **Baking into batters or doughs**: Not recommended, and it makes dosing impossible to control.

### The Golden Rule

**When in doubt, season at the end.** Finishing a plate with a sachet gives every guest control over their own serving.

### Dosing Guidance

Each sachet is planned as one 5mg serving. Final dosing guidance will follow the product label. Do not split or combine sachets until the label says how. If you are new to edibles, start low, wait at least 90 minutes before having more, and always tell guests the dose.

## Best Uses for THC Seasoning

### Ideal Applications

1. **Finishing plates** - pasta, bowls, eggs, roasted vegetables
2. **At the table** - pass sachets and let guests season their own
3. **Fruit and snacks** - chili-lime on mango, watermelon, popcorn
4. **Drink rims** - chili-lime on a margarita or michelada rim
5. **Desserts** - chili-lime dusted on dark chocolate

### What to Avoid

- Seasoning before high-heat cooking
- Stirring into dishes still on the burner
- Recipes where the seasoning disappears into a batter or sauce (dose becomes untraceable)
- Leaving sachets where guests can mistake them for ordinary seasoning

## Tips for Dinner Parties

1. **Label everything clearly** - never serve THC dishes without disclosure
2. **Offer alternatives** - always have a non-infused version available
3. **Start low, go slow** - especially with new guests
4. **Time your courses** - edibles take 30-90 minutes to onset
5. **Plan the evening** - ensure guests can get home safely
