## The Flavor Challenge

Let's be honest: traditional cannabis edibles often taste... like cannabis. That "weedy" flavor can overwhelm a dish, which is why so many edibles hide behind chocolate or sugar.

Grown sachets are meant to be different. The goal is a real seasoning blend without the strong cannabis taste. Pairing still matters, though. The right dish makes each blend taste intentional.

## Cajun Seasoning: Flavor Profile

Grown Cajun Seasoning is a savory Cajun-style blend, built for everyday cooking:

- Savory, warm spice (flavor and heat level still in development)
- Goal: no cannabis flavor

**Think of it as:** The Cajun shaker you would use anyway, with a 5mg upgrade.

### Natural Partners

**Chicken**
- Roasted thighs, wings, fried chicken
- Season right after it comes out of the oven

**Potatoes**
- Wedges, roasted potatoes, fries
- Season while they are still hot

**Shrimp**
- Seared in a skillet, grilled, or boiled
- Season at the table

**Burgers and vegetables**
- Smash burgers, roasted peppers, corn, zucchini
- A finishing dusting, never cooked in

## Chili-Lime Seasoning: Flavor Profile

Grown Chili-Lime Seasoning brings heat and citrus:

- Bright lime acidity
- Warm chili heat
- Savory salt base

**Think of it as:** The tajin-style move, dosed.

### Natural Partners

**Fruit**
- Mango, watermelon, pineapple, oranges
- The classic street-fruit pairing

**Elote and corn**
- Street corn, corn on the cob, cornbread
- Built for this

**Tacos and handhelds**
- Fish tacos, al pastor, lettuce wraps
- Dust right before the first bite

**The grill**
- Shrimp, chicken wings, grilled vegetables
- Heat loves heat

**Drink rims**
- Margaritas, micheladas, palomas
- Wet the rim with lime, dip, done

**Chocolate**
- Dark chocolate truffles, brownies
- Sweet-heat-citrus is a centuries-old combination

**Snacks**
- Popcorn, roasted nuts, chips and guacamole
- The bowl that disappears first

## Flavors That Bridge Both Blends

Some ingredients flatter whichever sachet you reach for:

- **Citrus** - brightens Cajun seasoning, doubles down on chili-lime
- **Garlic** - the universal base note
- **Cheese** - butter with the Cajun seasoning, cotija with chili-lime
- **Honey** - drizzle with either for sweet-savory contrast
- **Fresh herbs** - cilantro with chili-lime, parsley and dill with Cajun seasoning

## Building a Menu

A simple hosting rule: **one blend per course, one sachet per person.**

- **Starter:** Non-THC. Let guests settle in.
- **Main:** Cajun Seasoning on the protein or side (THC optional per plate).
- **Dessert:** Chili-Lime dusted fruit (THC optional per serving).

Two sachets across a whole evening is 10mg. Plan the night around that.

## What to Avoid

- **Delicate dishes** - a subtle poached fish doesn't need chili heat
- **Already-salty dishes** - taste before you season; the blend adds salt
- **Cooking it in** - both blends are finishers; heat before serving degrades THC
