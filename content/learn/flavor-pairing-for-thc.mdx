## The Flavor Challenge

Let's be honest: traditional cannabis edibles often taste... like cannabis. That "weedy" flavor can overwhelm a dish, which is why so many edibles hide behind chocolate or sugar.

Grown sachets are different. Our formulation process eliminates the strong cannabis taste, so what you get is a real seasoning blend. Pairing still matters, though. The right dish makes each blend taste intentional.

## Seasoning Salt: Flavor Profile

Grown Seasoning Salt is savory and herby, built to finish everyday food:

- Clean salinity
- Savory, herbaceous depth
- No cannabis flavor

**Think of it as:** The finishing seasoning you'd use anyway, with a 5mg upgrade.

### Natural Partners

**Eggs**
- Fried, poached, soft-scrambled, omelets
- The fastest upgrade in the lineup

**Potatoes**
- Crispy smashed, roasted, fries, hash browns
- Salt and potato is a forever pairing

**Roasted vegetables**
- Zucchini, peppers, carrots, broccoli
- Season after roasting, never before

**Pasta and grains**
- Pesto, aglio e olio, grain bowls, risotto
- Stir through the finished bowl

**Grilled proteins**
- Steak, chicken, lamb, pork, salmon
- Sprinkle after the rest, right before serving

**Fresh and creamy**
- Avocado toast, tomatoes with mozzarella, yogurt dips
- The herbs carry these dishes

## Chili-Lime Seasoning: Flavor Profile

Grown Chili-Lime Seasoning brings heat and citrus:

- Bright lime acidity
- Warm chili heat
- Savory salt base

**Think of it as:** The tajin-style move, dosed.

### Natural Partners

**Fruit**
- Mango, watermelon, pineapple, oranges
- The classic street-fruit pairing

**Elote and corn**
- Street corn, corn on the cob, cornbread
- Built for this

**Tacos and handhelds**
- Fish tacos, al pastor, lettuce wraps
- Dust right before the first bite

**The grill**
- Shrimp, chicken wings, grilled vegetables
- Heat loves heat

**Drink rims**
- Margaritas, micheladas, palomas
- Wet the rim with lime, dip, done

**Chocolate**
- Dark chocolate truffles, brownies
- Sweet-heat-citrus is a centuries-old combination

**Snacks**
- Popcorn, roasted nuts, chips and guacamole
- The bowl that disappears first

## Flavors That Bridge Both Blends

Some ingredients flatter whichever sachet you reach for:

- **Citrus** - brightens savory salt, doubles down on chili-lime
- **Garlic** - the universal base note
- **Cheese** - Parmesan with the salt, cotija with chili-lime
- **Honey** - drizzle with either for sweet-savory contrast
- **Fresh herbs** - cilantro with chili-lime, parsley and dill with the salt

## Building a Menu

A simple hosting rule: **one blend per course, one sachet per person.**

- **Starter:** Non-THC. Let guests settle in.
- **Main:** Seasoning Salt on the protein or side (THC optional per plate).
- **Dessert:** Chili-Lime dusted truffles or fruit (THC optional per serving).

Two sachets across a whole evening is 10mg. Plan the night around that.

## What to Avoid

- **Delicate dishes** - a subtle poached fish doesn't need chili heat
- **Already-salty dishes** - taste before you season; the blend is the salt
- **Cooking it in** - both blends are finishers; heat before serving degrades THC
