## Why Finish at the Table?

A spice blend is often cooked into the dish. That spreads it evenly, but you cannot take it back and you cannot tell who got what. Grown Cajun Seasoning is made the other way around: cook the food plain, then finish each plate with a sachet.

Each single-serve sachet is planned at 5mg of THC, so the last step is also the simplest one.

## What You Get

### Grown Cajun Seasoning (working name)

- A savory Cajun-style seasoning (formulation and heat level in development)
- Single-serve sachet, 5mg THC

Each sachet is planned as one 5mg serving. Dosing guidance will follow the final label. This is a draft: until the formulation is tested, do not split a sachet or combine several, and follow the product label.

## Best Applications for Cajun Seasoning

### Perfect Pairings

1. **Chicken** - roasted thighs or wings, seasoned right after cooking
2. **Potatoes** - wedges or fries while they are still hot
3. **Vegetables** - roasted peppers, corn and zucchini
4. **Burgers** - dusted on the patty just before the bun
5. **Shrimp** - seared in a skillet, seasoned at the table
6. **Eggs** - a pinch over scrambled or fried eggs

### When NOT to Use It

- Before roasting, frying, or grilling (this is a finisher, not a cooking ingredient)
- In cooking water or mixed into batters and doughs
- In dishes that will be reheated
- Anywhere you cannot control who eats what

## Tips for Entertaining

### The Pass-the-Sachet Method

The cleanest way to serve a mixed group:
1. Cook the whole meal with regular seasoning
2. Put sachets on the table in a clearly labeled dish
3. Each guest seasons their own plate, or skips it

This empowers guests to:
- Choose whether to use a sachet at all
- Skip THC entirely if preferred
- See exactly what everyone else is having

### Clear Communication

Always announce:
- "These sachets contain THC"
- The dose per sachet (5mg)
- Which dishes, if any, already contain THC

Never surprise your guests.

Grown Cajun Seasoning is a savory Cajun-style blend built for everyday cooking:

The sachet is planned as one serving. Sprinkle it across the plate, to taste.
