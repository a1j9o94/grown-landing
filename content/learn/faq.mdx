## About Our Products

### How much THC is in Grown Oil?

Grown Heirloom Oil contains **5mg THC per teaspoon** (15mg per tablespoon). Each 8oz bottle contains approximately 240mg total THC and provides about 48 teaspoon servings.

### How much THC is in Grown Salt?

Grown Flake Salt contains approximately **2mg THC per pinch**. Each jar provides 60+ servings.

### Does THC oil taste like cannabis?

No. Our formulation process eliminates the "weedy" taste common in other cannabis products. Grown Oil tastes like a high-quality olive oil blend with no detectable cannabis flavor.

### Are Grown products tested?

Yes. Every batch is third-party tested for potency, purity, and safety. We test for THC/CBD content, pesticides, heavy metals, and microbial contamination.

## Cooking & Usage

### Will cooking destroy the THC?

THC begins to degrade at temperatures above 320°F (160°C). For best results, add THC oil after cooking or use it in low-heat applications. We recommend treating it as a finishing oil.

For detailed guidance, see our [Cooking with THC Oil](/learn/cooking-with-thc-oil) guide.

### Can I cook with THC salt?

THC salt is designed as a finishing salt, not a cooking salt. Adding it to boiling water or cooking it into dishes will reduce potency and eliminate the texture benefits. Always add salt after cooking is complete.

### What's the best way to use the oil?

Treat Grown Oil as a premium finishing oil:
- Drizzle over finished pasta
- Whisk into salad dressings
- Toss with roasted vegetables after cooking
- Add to soups just before serving

See [When to Add THC Oil](/learn/when-to-add-thc-oil) for more strategies.

## Dosing & Effects

### How long until I feel the effects?

**THC oil:** Typically 45-90 minutes for effects to begin, with peak effects at 2-3 hours.

**THC salt:** Can work faster (15-30 minutes) due to sublingual absorption when crystals dissolve on your tongue.

For complete timing information, see [Edibles Timing Basics](/learn/edibles-timing-basics).

### What's a good starting dose?

For new users, we recommend starting with **2.5-5mg THC**. Wait at least 90 minutes before consuming more. Most experienced users find 5-10mg to be a comfortable dose.

### How long do effects last?

Effects typically last 4-8 hours depending on dose, tolerance, and individual metabolism. Peak effects occur around 2-3 hours after consumption.

## Storage & Shelf Life

### How should I store Grown products?

**Oil:** Store in a cool, dark place or refrigerate for extended freshness.
**Salt:** Store at room temperature in a dry location.

Keep both products sealed when not in use and always out of reach of children and pets.

### How long do the products last?

**Oil:** Unopened lasts 12+ months; opened oil is best used within 6 months.
**Salt:** Unopened lasts 18+ months; opened salt maintains quality for about 12 months.

See [Storage & Shelf Life](/learn/storage-shelf-life-thc-oil-salt) for detailed guidance.

## Availability & Legal

### Where is Grown available?

Grown products are currently **only available in Colorado**. We're focused on perfecting our Colorado launch before expanding to additional states.

### Is Grown legal?

Yes, in Colorado. Our products are produced and sold in compliance with Colorado cannabis regulations. THC products cannot be transported across state lines.

### Can I give Grown products as a gift?

Yes, within Colorado, to adults 21+. Products cannot be gifted across state lines. Always ensure recipients are of legal age.

## Health & Safety

### Can I use Grown products if I'm taking medication?

Consult your healthcare provider. THC can interact with certain medications. If you're on prescription drugs, especially those metabolized by the liver, check with your doctor first.

### Are Grown products vegan/gluten-free?

Yes. Both Grown Oil and Grown Salt are vegan, gluten-free, and contain no major allergens. The oil is made from olive and coconut oils; the salt is pure sea salt.

### What if I consume too much?

Stay calm—effects are temporary. Find a comfortable space, stay hydrated, and wait. Effects will gradually subside. See [Responsible Use](/legal/responsible-use) for more guidance.
