## About Our Products

### How much THC is in a Grown sachet?

Every Grown sachet contains exactly **5mg of THC** and about 3g of seasoning (roughly 1/2 teaspoon). One sachet is one serving. Each box contains 5 sachets (25mg total).

### What's the difference between the two blends?

**Seasoning Salt** is savory and herby, built as an everyday finisher for eggs, vegetables, pasta, and grilled proteins. **Chili-Lime Seasoning** brings heat and citrus, made for rims, elote, fruit, and the grill. Both are 5mg THC per sachet.

### Does Grown seasoning taste like cannabis?

No. Our formulation process eliminates the "weedy" taste common in other cannabis products. Each blend tastes like what it is: a well-made seasoning.

### Are Grown products tested?

Yes. Every batch is third-party tested for potency, purity, and safety. We test for THC/CBD content, pesticides, heavy metals, and microbial contamination.

## Cooking & Usage

### Will cooking destroy the THC?

Prolonged high heat (320°F and above) degrades THC. For best results, season after cooking or sprinkle on warm plates. Never add sachets before roasting, frying, grilling, or baking.

For detailed guidance, see our [Cooking with THC Seasoning](/learn/cooking-with-thc-seasoning) guide.

### Can I cook with Grown seasoning?

Grown seasoning is designed as a finishing seasoning, not a cooking ingredient. Add it after cooking so potency stays intact and every serving stays traceable.

### What's the best way to use the sachets?

Treat each sachet as a single serving:
- Sprinkle over a finished plate
- Stir through a warm bowl off heat
- Dust over fruit, snacks, or dessert
- Rim a glass with chili-lime

See [When to Add THC Seasoning](/learn/when-to-add-thc-seasoning) for more strategies.

## Dosing & Effects

### How long until I feel the effects?

Typically **30-90 minutes** for effects to begin, with peak effects at 2-3 hours. Onset is faster on an empty stomach and slower after a large meal.

For complete timing information, see [Edibles Timing Basics](/learn/edibles-timing-basics).

### What's a good starting dose?

For new users, we recommend starting with **half to one sachet (2.5-5mg THC)**. Wait at least 90 minutes before consuming more. Most experienced users find 5-10mg to be a comfortable dose.

### How long do effects last?

Effects typically last 4-8 hours depending on dose, tolerance, and individual metabolism. Peak effects occur around 2-3 hours after consumption.

## Storage & Shelf Life

### How should I store Grown products?

Keep sachets sealed in their box, in a **cool, dark, dry place** like a pantry cupboard. Keep away from the stove and sink, and always out of reach of children and pets.

### How long do the products last?

Sealed sachets are best within **12 months of the pack date** on the box. Once a sachet is open, use it right away.

See [Storage & Shelf Life](/learn/storage-and-shelf-life) for detailed guidance.

## Availability & Legal

### Where is Grown available?

Grown products are currently **only available in Illinois**. We're focused on perfecting our Illinois launch before expanding to additional states.

### Is Grown legal?

Yes, in Illinois. Our products are produced and sold in compliance with Illinois cannabis regulations. THC products cannot be transported across state lines.

### Can I give Grown products as a gift?

Yes, within Illinois, to adults 21+. Products cannot be gifted across state lines. Always ensure recipients are of legal age.

## Health & Safety

### Can I use Grown products if I'm taking medication?

Consult your healthcare provider. THC can interact with certain medications. If you're on prescription drugs, especially those metabolized by the liver, check with your doctor first.

### Are Grown products vegan/gluten-free?

Yes. Both Grown blends are vegan and gluten-free, with no major allergens.

### What if I consume too much?

Stay calm. Effects are temporary. Find a comfortable space, stay hydrated, and wait. Effects will gradually subside. See [Responsible Use](/legal/responsible-use) for more guidance.
