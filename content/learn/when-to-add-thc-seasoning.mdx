## The Timing Question

"When should I add the sachet?" is one of the most common questions we hear. The answer depends on what you are cooking, how hot it gets, and who is eating.

Here is the framework we use.

## The Three Timing Strategies

### 1. Finish (Recommended for Most Dishes)

**Season each plate after cooking is complete.**

This is the safest and most reliable approach. No heat exposure keeps it simple, and every guest controls their own serving.

**Best for:**
- Pasta and grain bowls
- Roasted vegetables (after they leave the oven)
- Grilled proteins (after they leave the grill)
- Eggs, avocado toast, pizza
- Tacos, elote, and anything handheld

**How to do it:**
1. Cook your dish normally with regular seasoning
2. Plate it
3. Sprinkle one sachet per THC serving
4. Serve immediately

### 2. Warm Toss

**Stir the sachet through a dish that is off heat but still warm.**

This works when you want the seasoning integrated rather than sprinkled, like a shared bowl of pasta where everyone at the table is having THC.

**Best for:**
- Shared pasta or grain dishes (THC guests only)
- Warm dips and mashed dishes
- Compound butter or yogurt sauces (off heat)

**How to do it:**
1. Take the dish fully off heat
2. Count your THC servings and add one sachet per serving
3. Stir thoroughly to distribute
4. Portion and serve

### 3. No-Cook

**Season dishes that never see heat at all.**

No heat, no timing concerns.

**Best for:**
- Fresh fruit (chili-lime on mango, watermelon, pineapple)
- Salads and crudo
- Drink rims
- Desserts (chili-lime on chocolate)
- Popcorn and snacks

## What to Avoid

### High Heat

Do not add THC seasoning before:
- **Roasting or baking** (sustained 350°F+)
- **Searing or frying**
- **Grilling** (direct flame)
- **Boiling** (it disperses into the water, and the dose is gone)

### Mixed-In Cooking

Avoid folding sachets into:
- Batters and doughs
- Long braises or stews
- Anything where you cannot trace which portion has THC

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Pasta Night

1. Cook pasta and sauce normally
2. Plate the bowls
3. **Sprinkle one sachet on each THC bowl**
4. Pass extra Parmesan, serve

### Example 2: Taco Night

1. Cook the filling with regular seasoning
2. Set out tortillas, toppings, and sachets
3. **Each guest seasons their own tacos** (follow the label for how much)
4. One sachet per guest keeps the count clean

### Example 3: Fruit Plate

**No heat needed:**
1. Slice mango, watermelon, or pineapple
2. Arrange on a platter
3. **Dust with chili-lime sachets** just before serving
4. Label the platter

## When Precision Matters Most

For dinner parties and shared meals:
- One sachet per person keeps the count exact
- Season plates, not the shared dish (unless everyone is in)
- Never let a sachet look like ordinary table seasoning
- Double-check who is having THC before you sprinkle
