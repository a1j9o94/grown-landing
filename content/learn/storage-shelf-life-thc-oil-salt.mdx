## Why Storage Matters

Proper storage protects two things:

1. **Potency** — THC degrades over time, especially with exposure to light, heat, and air
2. **Freshness** — Oil can go rancid; salt can absorb moisture and clump

Store your Grown products correctly, and they'll deliver consistent experiences for months.

## THC Oil Storage

### Ideal Conditions

- **Temperature:** Cool room temperature (60-70°F) or refrigerated
- **Light:** Store in dark place or opaque container (our bottle is designed for this)
- **Air:** Keep bottle sealed when not in use

### Refrigeration

**Should you refrigerate THC oil?**

Optional but beneficial. Refrigeration:
- Extends shelf life significantly
- Slows oxidation and THC degradation
- May cause oil to thicken (let it warm to room temp before using)

If refrigerating:
- Let oil come to room temperature before measuring (cold oil pours differently)
- 15-20 minutes on the counter is usually enough

### What Degrades THC Oil

| Factor | Impact | Solution |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Light | Breaks down THC | Dark storage |
| Heat | Accelerates degradation | Cool location |
| Air | Oxidizes cannabinoids | Keep sealed |
| Time | Gradual potency loss | Use within timeframe |

### Shelf Life

**Unopened:** 12+ months from production date
**Opened:** 6 months for best quality, up to 9 months if stored properly

After this time, oil won't be dangerous—but potency and flavor may decline.

### Signs of Degraded Oil

- Off or rancid smell
- Noticeably weaker effects than expected
- Changed color (significantly darker)
- Unusual taste

If you notice these signs, it's time for a fresh bottle.

## THC Salt Storage

### Ideal Conditions

- **Temperature:** Room temperature (don't refrigerate)
- **Humidity:** Low humidity environment
- **Container:** Airtight when not in use

### The Moisture Problem

Salt is hygroscopic—it absorbs moisture from the air. Moisture causes:
- Clumping
- Dissolution of flake structure
- Potential for mold (in extreme cases)

**Prevention:**
- Keep jar sealed except when using
- Don't store near the stove, dishwasher, or sink
- If using in humid environments, consider a small silica packet in the jar

### Shelf Life

**Unopened:** 18+ months
**Opened:** 12 months for best texture and quality

Salt doesn't "go bad" the way oil does, but texture and potency can decline over time.

### Signs of Degraded Salt

- Clumping or solid mass (moisture exposure)
- Loss of flake structure
- Noticeably weaker effects
- Off smells (rare, indicates contamination)

If clumped but otherwise fine, you can break it apart—potency may still be okay, but texture is compromised.

## Childproofing & Safety

This is non-negotiable. THC products must be stored:

- **Out of reach of children and pets**
- **In original, labeled containers**
- **Separate from regular cooking ingredients**
- **In a locked cabinet if children are present**

### Label Everything

Even if you're the only one who cooks:
- Keep products in original containers
- Never transfer to unlabeled bottles
- Consider a separate "THC shelf" in your pantry

### Guest Awareness

If you have guests who might access your kitchen:
- Store THC products completely out of sight
- Brief guests on what's in your kitchen
- Never leave THC products on the counter during parties

## Travel & Transport

### Can You Travel with THC Products?

**Within Colorado:** Yes, in your vehicle with limits on total THC
**Across state lines:** No—illegal regardless of destination state laws
**On airplanes:** No—federal jurisdiction applies

### If Transporting Locally

- Keep in trunk or locked compartment
- Maintain original packaging
- Don't open containers while driving
